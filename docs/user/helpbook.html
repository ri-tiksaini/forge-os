3. Keeping Your Dev Machine Fast & Clean

Zero-RAM Service Parking

Databases like PostgreSQL, Redis, and MariaDB are parked behind systemd-socket-proxyd. They consume 0 MB RAM until your app connects, and automatically stop 90 seconds after disconnect.

One-Click RAM & Dev Cleaner

Click the broom icon on the bottom bar or run sudo forge-clean to flush dirty buffers, drop kernel page/dentry caches, trim logs, and clear pip/npm/go/apt caches safely. Use --deep to prune unused Podman images.

Safety Net & Recovery

Daily allowlisted backups protect /etc and your dotfiles without archiving giant browser caches. Run forge-repair configs or forge-repair full anytime to restore a clean state.
